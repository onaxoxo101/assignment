The overlap

What chess taught me
about code

◇

Look ahead

Sketch the structure before writing markup, the same way you pick a plan before touching a piece.

◈

Patterns repeat

Forks and pins, flex rows and grid areas — once you've seen a shape enough times you stop calculating it from scratch.

◉

Review your losses

The game you lost teaches more than three you won. Same with the bug that took all evening.
