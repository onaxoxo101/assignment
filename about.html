Our Story

From A Garage Gym To The City's Best

Pulse Fitness started in 2015 with a single squat rack and a big dream: make world-class training accessible to everyone. Today we're a 20,000 sq ft facility serving over 12,000 members — but that founding spirit hasn't changed.

We believe fitness is for every body. No egos, no judgment — just relentless support to help you become the strongest version of yourself.
