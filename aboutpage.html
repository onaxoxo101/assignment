A little background

I'm 20, currently in my 300 level at Babcock University, and I spend most of my free time learning to build things for the web. My faith matters a great deal to me and shapes how I work — carefully, and with people in mind.

Right now I'm learning web development and Python, and picking up trading as a second skill. Further down the line I'd love to move into animation — drawing and animated films were my first love, and design work scratches the same itch. Outside of that: music constantly, chess often, and enough of both introvert and extrovert to enjoy a quiet afternoon and a loud room equally.
