Why she's my mentor

Adachukwu built two demanding careers at once — medicine and business — and did it without letting either one slip. She grew up in Lagos, worked her way to a medical degree with top honours from the University of Lagos, and went into healthcare because she wanted the work to actually relieve something for somebody.

What I take from her isn't the CV. It's the habit underneath it: decide what you want to be good at, then put in the unglamorous hours until you are. That's the approach I'm trying to apply to development — steady practice over shortcuts.
